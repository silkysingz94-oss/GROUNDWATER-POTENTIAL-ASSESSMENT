METHODOLOGY & LIMITATIONS

Elevation is extracted using Open-Meteo. Slope is an approximate gradient derived from neighbouring sample elevations; it is not a proper DEM slope raster.

Rainfall is the mean of annual sums of daily precipitation over the selected reference period.

Soil sand content is extracted from SoilGrids at 0–5 cm depth where the service responds.

Drainage proximity and density use mapped OpenStreetMap waterways. Network completeness varies.

Flow accumulation, LULC suitability, JRC surface-water occurrence and lineament density are excluded until appropriate datasets are connected.

The index is preliminary screening, not a validated aquifer map or borehole-yield prediction.
