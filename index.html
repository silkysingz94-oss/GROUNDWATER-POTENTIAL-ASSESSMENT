METHODOLOGY & LIMITATIONS
Administrative boundaries are loaded from the Gambia Census EA GeoJSON. Region, LGA, district and ward filters are generated from its attributes.

Elevation and rainfall are retrieved from public web services. Slope is estimated from neighbouring sample elevations. Flow accumulation, drainage proximity, drainage density and terrain-edge density are simplified spatial proxies.

Soil sand, LULC and surface-water occurrence are not included until reliable public raster data can be retrieved.

The result is a preliminary screening index, not a validated aquifer map or borehole-yield prediction.
